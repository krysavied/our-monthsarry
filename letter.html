A LITTLE SOMETHING FROM MY HEART

My Letter To You 💌

My Honey,

Happy first monthsary to us! 💗

I honestly don't know how to put everything I feel into words, but I want you to know how grateful I am that I get to call you mine.

This first month may seem short, but every conversation, every laugh, every little update, every moment we spent together has become something special to me.

Thank you for being patient with me, especially during the moments when I don't know how to express what I'm feeling. Thank you for staying, for caring, for making an effort, and for reminding me that I don't have to face everything alone.

I know we're not perfect. We might misunderstand each other sometimes, and there will probably be difficult days ahead. But I hope that whenever those days come, we'll choose to understand each other, communicate, forgive, and stay.

I want more memories with you. More random conversations, more laughs, more pictures, more adventures, and more ordinary days that somehow become my favorite days simply because you're there.

Thank you for being you, Honey.

Here's to our first month, and hopefully to many more months and memories together. ♡

I love you so much. 💗

— Your favorite person ♡
